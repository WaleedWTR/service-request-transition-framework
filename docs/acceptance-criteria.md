# Acceptance Criteria

A request type is ready to transition when:

- process owner is agreed
- resolver group is confirmed
- roles and permissions are available
- end-to-end test completed successfully
- knowledge article is approved
- known exceptions are documented
- SLA / OLA expectations are clear
- escalation path is documented
- reporting is available
- training / walkthrough completed
- rollback or fallback path is understood
- transition owner formally accepts readiness

## Exit from hypercare

Hypercare can close when:

- volumes are stable
- no critical knowledge gaps remain
- avoidable reassignments are within agreed tolerance
- SLA performance is stable
- open transition defects have owners and dates
